About

Between the business and the data

I’m a London-based Business Analyst with over 10 years of experience in business management and operations, including 5+ years in business analysis and co-founding a supply chain business.

I have worked across logistics, petroleum supply chain and government, including co-founding and running Cape Atlantic Global Services. My work combines process mapping and root-cause analysis with hands-on data work in SQL, Power BI and Advanced Excel. I have improved the accuracy of operational and financial records by 15% and cut time-to-hire by 25%, and I enjoy being the person who gets business and technical teams on the same page.

I am comfortable moving between a stakeholder workshop and a dataset. I write business requirements, user stories and acceptance criteria, and I use data to back recommendations with evidence.

Having worked in petroleum supply chain, I have also built on that with a certification in Renewable Energy Business Development, and I’m interested in how energy transition, sustainability and good process design connect. That said, the problems I solve are universal: whether it is a tech company, a hotel group or a multinational, unclear requirements and inefficient processes cost money everywhere.

How I approach a business problem

  1. Understand the problem

    Find the real issue behind the symptoms, using root-cause techniques like 5 Whys and Fishbone.

  2. Engage stakeholders

    Identify who is affected, map power and interest, and agree who is responsible (RACI).

  3. Define requirements

    Turn needs into business, functional and non-functional requirements, user stories and acceptance criteria.

  4. Analyse & recommend

    Compare options against clear criteria and back the recommendation with data and costs.

  5. Measure the result

    Set KPIs and success measures so the business can see whether the change worked.
